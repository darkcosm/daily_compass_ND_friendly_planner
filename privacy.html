Privacy

Daily Compass keeps what you type on this device. The notes sit in localStorage in this browser, on this device. They are never sent to a server.

There is no account. There are no cookies. There is no analytics or other tracking. The page does not load anything from a third party.

If you clear this site's data in your browser, the entries are deleted. Export writes a JSON file so you can keep a copy of your own.
